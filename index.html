About the project

Bill Calendar: Payday Planner is an independently developed Android personal-finance application. The goal is to help users plan recurring obligations around payday and make better-informed day-to-day spending decisions. The app is currently in active development and is not yet publicly available. Planned bank connectivity will use a third-party financial data provider; users will be able to choose whether to connect eligible accounts.
